About the Event

On May 28, 2026, California's contractors, public works agencies, and industry leaders converge at the Hilton Anaheim for UCON's 5th Annual Public Works Summit — the state's premier one-day event connecting the people who plan, fund, and build California's infrastructure.

"The Public Works Summit puts California's contractors face-to-face with the agencies shaping the state's infrastructure future." — Emily Cohen, CEO, United Contractors
