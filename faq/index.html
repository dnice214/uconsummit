About the Summit
The UCON Public Works Summit is a premier convening of California's public works community, bringing together contractors, public agencies, unions, and industry partners for insight, collaboration, and relationship building. Now in its 5th year, it is the state's must-attend event for anyone working in California's public infrastructure sector.
